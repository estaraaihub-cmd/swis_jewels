# SWIS JEWELS — Karathur, Tirur, Kerala

A 9-page luxury jewellery website built for **SWIS JEWELS** (Karathur, Tirur, Kerala, India), featuring its official **SWA DIAMONDS** showcase.

## Pages Included
1. `index.html` — **Home**: Full-screen hero, featured categories, SWA DIAMONDS showcase, bridal showcase, new arrivals, why choose SWIS JEWELS, showroom introduction, visual journal, store visit CTA, and footer.
2. `collections.html` — **Collections**: All 12 jewellery categories, live search, category filter pills, sorting, and responsive product grid.
3. `product.html` — **Product Details**: Multi-image gallery with click-to-zoom lightbox, reference/SKU, specifications/enquiry guidance, pre-filled WhatsApp enquiry link, and related products.
4. `swa-diamonds.html` — **SWA DIAMONDS**: Dedicated diamond campaign showcase covering Diamond Rings, Necklaces, Earrings, Bracelets, Bridal Diamond Collections, and featured pieces.
5. `bridal.html` — **Bridal Collection**: Traditional Kerala bridal gold sets, wedding ornaments, bridal diamond pieces, and interactive inspiration gallery.
6. `about.html` — **About SWIS JEWELS**: Brand overview, SWA DIAMONDS association, showroom interiors/exterior, and Karathur location info.
7. `gallery.html` — **Gallery**: Filterable editorial gallery with full-screen keyboard-accessible lightbox.
8. `offers.html` — **Offers & Updates**: Owner-managed promotional announcements, new arrivals, and seasonal campaigns.
9. `contact.html` — **Contact & Visit**: Storefront photo, Karathur Tirur address, contact/hours/Instagram placeholders, Google Maps slot, Get Directions link, and validated contact enquiry form.

## Local Preview
Run `server.ps1` from `swis-jewels/` (PowerShell) or open `index.html` in a browser. See `CMS_INTEGRATION.md` for replacing the official SWIS logo, storefront photo, and contact placeholders.
